# VEEP (Vinyl Bee Beep Player)

A premium audio player that blends the nostalgic soul of vinyl with a sharp, modern "Bee" aesthetic.

## 🐝 Concept
Based on the keywords from `concept`:
- **Vinyl**: Classic turntable mechanics and analog warmth.
- **Bee / Beep**: A high-contrast, energetic visual identity.
- **Player**: Focused on high-quality `.wav` playback.

## 🎨 Visual Identity
- **Color Palette**: Deep **Black**, Vibrant **Yellow**, and Metallic **Gold**.
- **The "Bee" Aesthetic**: Sharp lines, high-contrast UI, and hive-like precision.

## ✨ Features
- **33.3 RPM Record Rotation**: Smooth, physics-based vinyl animation.
- **Stinger Tonearm**: A mechanical stylus that moves with precision when playback starts.
- **Honey-Visualizer**: Real-time frequency bars reacting to your `.wav` files.
- **Premium Glassmorphism**: Interactive controls with polished, modern transparency.

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Load**: Drag and drop or select `.wav` files.
- **Play/Pause**: Click the record or hit `Space`.
- **Volume**: Precision honey-gold slider.
